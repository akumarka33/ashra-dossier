CHARACTER DOSSIER

ASHRA

HEIGHT: 175 CM
BUILD: LEAN / HYPER-ELASTIC
TEMPERAMENT: COLD PRAGMATIST
Core Motivation

Ashra does not seek a cure or deliverance from the beast — he accepted long ago that the Hound will remain with him until the end. His goal is simpler and harsher: never let the monster devour him from within, yet never become someone who fears his own strength. He holds the leash out of stubbornness and cold calculation — for him, every fight is not a battle for victory, but another test of who breaks whom.

Appearance & Demeanor

Physique

175 cm. A dry, lean, and hyper-elastic frame built on spring-like ligaments, dense musculature, and zero excess weight.

Head & Gaze

Tousled black hair (wolf cut) with his face sunk in deep shadow. A heavy, piercing stare of bright crimson eyes — during sudden dashes in motion, his eyes leave a brief red trail slicing through the dark.

Organic Claws

Natural, organic black hands tipped with razor-sharp claws extending slightly past the wrists, as if carved from hardened shadow or chitin.

Behavioral Contrast

Restrained, cold, pragmatic, and slightly detached in daily life under strict self-control. In combat, he drops all civility, moving with the ruthless, brutal efficiency of a starving predator.

Origin & The Hound

Curse of the Devourer

In the past, Ashra survived an encounter with an ancient shadow hound-devourer — a gaunt, solitary, and vicious beast — suppressing and absorbing the monster's essence from within.

Predatory Focus & Feeding

Unburdened by sensory overload in everyday life, once Ashra locks onto a target he effortlessly reads fear, ragged breathing, and subtle muscle tension before a strike. In the heat of combat, the devourer feeds on enemies' fresh blood, sealing minor wounds and stripping away fatigue while the fight lasts.

Bond & Price of the Leash

The Hound speaks no words — it answers with a low growl in his chest, flashes of alien imagery, bloodlust, and sudden spikes of adrenaline. Ashra holds the chain himself and never blacks out completely. Yet in brutal moments, he may intentionally loosen his grip — or it slips under pressure, letting the Hound cut in for seconds (his crimson pupils narrow into beastly slits and shadows thicken around his armor). Under its sway, he ignores his own defense, charges head-on, and strikes with excess brutality. The beast remembers freedom: each time it pulls harder, leaving Ashra drained and ravenous afterward. He knows well that if he ever lets go entirely, only a hollow shell obsessed with an endless hunt will remain.

Wardrobe & Armament

Civilian Attire

A long black gothic coat with a high stand-up collar, silver clasps, a waist belt, and wide cuffs that completely conceal his black clawed hands. This is a separate peacetime outfit worn instead of his combat base and armor — the coat is never worn over the exoskeleton, but swapped out prior to battle.

Combat Base

Form-fitting black long-sleeve turtleneck accented with thin crimson stripes, black drawstring shorts, and dense dark-gray leg wraps for unrestricted agility and silent movement.

Armor of the Beast

A thin, hyper-sculpted, dense black exoskeleton featuring a spiked spine ridge (the materialized shell of the absorbed hound) and a tattered black mane-cloak. Far lighter than knight plate so it never hinders acrobatics, yet dense enough to lend tangible weight to every impact. Ashra dons or removes the predatory beast helmet-mask freely at will.

Weapons

Two curved predatory daggers with organic black hilts and matte dark blades that cast zero glare in the dark.

Combat Mechanics

"Gouging" Acrobatics

Grounded, predatory physicality incorporating quadrupedal sprints, low rolls, and wall-jumps. Ashra never floats through the air defying physics — he weaponizes gravity, leverage, and the battering-ram inertia of his dense frame, tearing deep gouges into earth and stone with claws and feet on every launch and landing.

Exoskeleton Output

The armor numbs pain and fear, stripping away the brain's limiters to push speed and reflexes to inhuman levels. If joints dislocate or tendons strain, the armor rigidly locks and binds his frame from within so he never loses momentum.

Cost of Overdrive

Thanks to his lean, elastic build, Ashra absorbs high-speed strain well in standard skirmishes. However, when he pushes speed and fury to the absolute peak, blood vessels rupture under the pressure — and dark blood seeps from the slits of his helmet and the seams of his armor.
